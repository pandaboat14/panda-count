Sample game: who holds what

Every roll starts from this same position so you can compare the three designs. A region pays its owner 1 of its resource whenever the dice total matches its number. On a 7, the ogres raid: anyone holding more than 9 resource cards loses half.

The game server rolls the numbers before the dice leave your hand. The throw itself is real physics, worked out in an instant and played back, so every player sees the same bounce and the dice always land on the server’s roll.
